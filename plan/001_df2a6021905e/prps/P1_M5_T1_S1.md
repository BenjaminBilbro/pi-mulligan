## Why

- This is the capstone wiring task of milestone M5 (spec/11 §8 Step 8). Every prior task (M1 foundation, M2 pure helpers, M3 Pi-coupled glue, M4 tools) produced independently-tested modules; NONE of them is reachable by Pi until `index.ts` registers them. Without this task the extension is a pile of unconnected modules.
- Establishes the zero-config contract (spec/09 §1, spec/11 §3 DoD #4): the extension MUST work with no `mulligan` settings block, because v1 has NO Pi-settings accessor (see Known Gotchas). `getConfig()` lazily caches validated `DEFAULT_CONFIG`; handlers read it on each fire.
- Locks in the D8/C2 constraints (spec/02): the factory MUST NOT use `pi.sendUserMessage`, `pi.registerCommand`, or `pi.registerShortcut` — a verifiable grep gate enforces this.

## What

A thin SYNC factory that delegates entirely to shipped modules. It creates no patterns of its own.
